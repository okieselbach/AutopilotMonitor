# Code of Conduct

This applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and the documentation repository.

## Expectations

- Be respectful and constructive. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent. Most confusion is a documentation gap, not malice.
- Keep discussions on topic and searchable: clear titles, reproducible steps, text instead of screenshots of text where possible.
- Never post customer data, tenant IDs, device identifiers, tokens, or log excerpts containing personal data in public issues or discussions. Redact first.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit, hide, or delete content and may block accounts that violate this code. To raise a concern, contact the maintainers privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/okieselbach) rather than in a public thread.
